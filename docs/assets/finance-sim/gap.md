Figure F1. Accuracy gap before and after adjustment, positive control (identifying versus anonymised FRED macro prompts). Data: the stored pre-registered analysis (task 7.10; read, not recomputed) of the full_run stage, model google/gemma-3-4b-it, plan f7603eeb8efd, pilot items excluded; per variant, the pre-cutoff minus post-cutoff difference of identifying minus anonymised accuracy (did_accuracy_gap), the no-adjustment variants beside the top-k and full-distribution adjustments. Serving arm (primary role, the pre-registered tests): 90 planned months (68 on or before the model's cutoff, 22 after); achieved power missing: fewer than two months carry the noise statistic before or after the cutoff, so the pooled standard deviation and the achieved power cannot be computed. Sampling arm (probe role, a small paired check): 20 planned months (15 on or before the model's cutoff, 5 after); achieved power missing: fewer than two months carry the noise statistic before or after the cutoff, so the pooled standard deviation and the achieved power cannot be computed. Achieved power is read as a t-test approximation under independent months: the one-sided pooled two-sample t test of simulated_power_two_sample at the pre-registered effect, with the realised pre-cutoff and post-cutoff month counts and the pooled within-period standard deviation of the noise variant's identifying minus anonymised correctness; the confirmatory test of the DiD gap is the two-sided moving-block bootstrap, which allows dependence between neighbouring months, so this power approximates the power of that test. Sample size: months per variant in the table (a month counts when both renderings carry the value; the months without it are counted per variant, with their reasons in the stored analysis). Interval: 95 percent non-circular moving-block percentile bootstrap over months, block length 6 months, 2000 resamples, seed 20261110; p raw is two-sided from the kept resamples, Holm-adjusted across the variants of each arm (alpha 0.05); the two period differences are measurements without a p-value. Encoding: marker shape gives the adjustment (circle no adjustment, square top-k, triangle full distribution), an open marker a single draw and a filled one the ensemble; each row is labelled; the vertical line marks no gap. Missing (not drawn): serving single_none, single_top_k, ensemble_none, ensemble_top_k: no planned month carries the value in both renderings; serving single_full_distribution, ensemble_full_distribution: missing for the serving arm: the full-distribution host serves the local GGUF, which are different weights from the served model, and values from different builds are not comparable (Requirement 9.4); sampling ensemble_none, ensemble_top_k, ensemble_full_distribution: 5 months carry the value in both renderings, fewer than the frozen block length of 6 months, so the moving-block bootstrap is not defined. A variant with dropped resamples had resamples whose needed period cell was empty (no pre-cutoff or no post-cutoff month among the resampled months); its interval and p-value are conditional on the kept resamples. Dropped resamples per variant are in the table (serving: up to 0 of 2000; sampling: up to 0 of 2000).
