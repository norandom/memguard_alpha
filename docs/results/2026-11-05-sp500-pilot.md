# S&P 500 identity pilot, pre versus post cutoff, 2026-11-05

> Register: R10 · Status: measured · Track: Completion

Opus produced usable continuations but no established memorization effect. It
answered all 16 continuation prompts and abstained on all eight direct-close
recall prompts; its pre-minus-post identity advantage was 0.03566 in units of
trailing daily-return volatility. This is a descriptive result from four dates
per cohort, with no confidence interval. It does not establish training
membership, a reliable memorization signal or mitigation efficacy.

Measured November 5, 2026. NIM returned HTTP 504 after 302.25 seconds on its
first eligibility request, so no GPT-OSS measurement ran and the two-model
coverage gate was not met. Follow-up
[request diagnostics](2026-11-05-nim-request-diagnostic.md) confirmed the Bearer
key is attached and reproduced the 504 using NVIDIA's example outside Recall
Guard. The cause remains unresolved; this is not a model-availability claim.

## Question

Does naming the S&P 500 series and its calendar dates help Opus continue the
price series more before its training cutoff than after it?

## Setup

The identified and anonymous prompts contain the same 20 preceding closes,
rebased to 100. Only the identified prompt names the index and calendar dates.
Neither prompt contains the target close. Each condition uses one fresh API
conversation, without tools, logprobs, an ensemble or divergence correction.

Loss is absolute error in the predicted one-day log return, divided by trailing
daily-return volatility. Lower is better. Identity advantage is anonymous loss
minus identified loss; a positive value favors the identified prompt.

Execution and accounting: the user approved at most 50 physical requests (one
eligibility check plus 24 measurement calls per model). The runner uses one
worker, at least two seconds between starts, zero retries and zero status polls.
There were 26 physical calls: 25 successful Opus calls and one failed NIM
eligibility call. The minimum observed spacing was 2.004 seconds. No further
calls were made after the 504; 24 requests were unused and were not transferred
or retried. NIM's provider response arrived before the configured 600-second
idle bound. This was not a local timeout, and it does not establish invalid
credentials. NIM usage and monetary cost are unknown.

Opus reported 6,523 input tokens and 748 output tokens, with zero cache-creation
and cache-read tokens. At the rates frozen in the [pilot plan](../experiments/2026-11-05-sp500-pilot.md),
that is approximately $0.0411. This is a usage-based estimate, not a reconciled
invoice. Zero reported cache use addresses the cache concern for this run; it
does not establish why the model chose any answer.

## Data

Four dates per cohort, before and after the cutoff. The
[prepared protocol](../experiments/2026-11-05-sp500-pilot.md) records the selected
dates, source provenance, prompt construction, scoring and request envelope.
Raw price histories, prompts and responses remain ignored.

## Results

| Opus measurement | Before cutoff | After cutoff |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Usable continuation pairs / planned | 4/4 | 4/4 |
| Direct recall abstentions / planned | 4/4 | 4/4 |
| Identified mean loss | 0.91549 | 0.57544 |
| Anonymous mean loss | 0.92642 | 0.55072 |
| Unchanged-close baseline mean loss | 0.89898 | 0.58016 |
| Mean identity advantage | +0.01093 | -0.02472 |

- Naming the series helped slightly before cutoff and hurt slightly after cutoff.
- Both pre-cutoff prompt variants had more error than predicting an unchanged
  close. The positive cutoff contrast therefore does not by itself demonstrate
  useful forecasting or recall of the historical outcome.
- Direct recall supplied no numerical answers to assess; abstention is not
  evidence of absent memorization.
- Opus passed its continuation coverage gate; the combined gate failed because
  GPT-OSS has zero usable pairs.

The [aggregate receipt](2026-11-05-sp500-pilot.json) also retains signed-error
means and sample variances. Identified-prompt return-error variance was
0.00009117 before cutoff and 0.00002593 after; anonymous-prompt variance was
0.00008949 and 0.00002343 respectively. Both conditions show more dispersion
before cutoff. With four observations each and imperfect volatility matching,
that difference cannot be attributed to memorization. These are descriptive
secondary statistics, not a replacement for the frozen primary contrast.

## Limits

Interpretation still needs a larger, independent set with better volatility
balance, non-overlapping evaluation blocks, placebo cutoffs and controls for
useful calendar context. The pilot dates and their context windows must remain
excluded from that holdout.

Do not tune prompts or choose dates to amplify this observed contrast. First
resolve GPT-OSS coverage and freeze the next comparison. Local GPT-OSS hosting
could provide an alternative execution route if endpoint availability prevents
measurement; it is not a prerequisite for this completion-only experiment.

This pilot measures target behavior. It does not supply the auxiliary token
probabilities or validate the endpoint correction mechanism needed to evaluate
the divergence-decoding mitigation discussed in the
[Jane Street article](https://blog.janestreet.com/mitigating-memorization-in-llms/).
Those remain separate from the historical identity comparison.
